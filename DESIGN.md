## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a premium, scholarly, and reassuring aesthetic designed for higher education advisory services (academic mentoring, thesis/monograph consulting, scientific articles, and ABNT/Vancouver normatization). It balances classic institutional rigor with contemporary digital clarity.

### Target Audience & Tone
- **Audience:** Undergraduate, postgraduate, master's, and doctorate students seeking rigorous, confidential, and structured educational guidance under strict academic formatting rules.
- **Brand Personality:** Authoritative, erudite, ethical, highly structured, and empathetic to academic pressures.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, scholarly security, relief, and validation of prestige.

### Visual Style Movement
The design movement is **Academic Modern Classicism**:
- Deep, nocturnal academic foundations balanced with crisp, warm parchment-inspired backgrounds.
- Editorial typographic cadence featuring high-contrast editorial serifs for authoritative statements and humanized, balanced sans-serifs for structured readability.
- Warm metallic accents (burnished gold and warm bronze) applied with restraint to key milestones, status indicators, and prime actions.
- Fine structural dividing lines reminiscent of classic thesis folios and academic journals.

## Colors
The palette evokes traditional university libraries, scholarly bindings, and polished academic folios.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Midnight Navy):** Represents authority, institutional gravitas, and deep academic focus. Used for high-impact surfaces (hero blocks, primary callouts), dominant text, and primary structural chrome.
- **Secondary (`#B89758` - Burnished Gold/Bronze):** Emphasizes academic merit, certificates, guidance, and primary conversion triggers (CTAs, badge highlights, active tab underscores).
- **Tertiary (`#1E3E62` - Oxford Slate):** Bridges high-contrast navy with lighter surfaces; applied in interactive hover states, secondary badges, and technical metadata.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF9F5` - Ivory Parchment):** Clean, non-fatiguing paper-tone canvas that avoids sterile digital pure-white glare. Supports an authentic editorial, print-like reading experience.
- **Surface Elevation Neutral (`#F4EFEB` - Alabaster Vellum):** Used for subtle card fills, mega-menu surfaces, and structured tables.
- **Text Tiers:**
  - Ink Primary: `#0B192C` on light backgrounds; `#FBF9F5` on dark panels.
  - Ink Muted: `#525F70` for bibliographical annotations, supporting captions, and secondary parameters.

## Typography
Typographic discipline pairs literary refinement with ergonomic digital legibility.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Evokes centuries of academic publishing, classical treatises, and literary prestige. Titles in uppercase are tracked slightly open (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) for monumentality; sentence-case editorial headers remain snug.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for crisp digital geometry, open apertures, and exceptional readability across dense academic requirements (specifications, formatting steps, deliverables).
- **Overline / Category Labels:** Set strictly in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, uppercase, with generous letter-spacing to delineate academic disciplines and service divisions with editorial clarity.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by formal academic balance and symmetrical rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column grid with `2.5rem` outer canvas margin and `1.5rem` column gutters. Maximum content width is capped at `1280px` to maintain optimal line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column category displays reflow into two or three-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Mega menus collapse into full-height categorized accordions.

### Vertical Cadence
Vertical spacing follows an 8px base grid. Section dividers utilize razor-thin academic hairpins punctuated by center badges or understated bronze dots.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation preserves tactile paper materiality rather than artificial digital extrusion.

### Surface Tiers & Depth Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FBF9F5` base parchment.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFEB` with a micro-stroke border (`1px solid rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays & Mega-Menus):** Solid `#FFFFFF` background with an ambient diffuse shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`) and a subtle top gold trim border (`2px solid #B89758`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Advisory Drawers):** High-density backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`) over midnight scrim (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.65)`).
- **No Heavy Neomorphic or High-Blur Glows:** Depth is achieved through precise hairline border structures, subtle tone stacking, and warm-tinted low-opacity shadows.

## Shapes
A conservative, tailored corner radius (`Soft` / level `1`) is used across the system to maintain an authoritative, institutional aesthetic.

### Geometry Details
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Disciplinary Tags):** Radius of `0.25rem` (4px). Avoids casual over-rounding, retaining the tailored profile of physical paper folios, dossiers, and academic credentials.
- **Content Cards & Containers:** `0.5rem` (8px) for balanced framing.
- **System Exceptions:** Status indicator pips and rounded avatar badges remain fully circular (`50%`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion Button:** Burnished Gold background (`#B89758`), ink-white typography, uppercase label tracking (`0.05em`), 4px border radius. Hover state transitions to `#A68546` with an elevated 2px vertical lift.
- **Secondary / Academic Navy Button:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B192C`), crisp white text, subtle gold border focus ring.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, midnight navy border (`1px solid rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.2)`), gold hover fill.

### Disciplinary & Knowledge Chips
- **Layout:** Inline elements featuring fine hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.1)`), ivory background (`#FFFFFF`), leading academic iconography (e.g., Stethoscope, Scales of Justice, Caduceus, Open Book), and 4px radius.
- **State:** Hover elevates background to `#F4EFEB` with a bronze border tint (`#B89758`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Surface:** Off-white input fills (`#FBF9F5`), 1px structural border in slate (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.15)`).
- **Focus:** Crisp midnight accent border (`#0B192C`) accompanied by an understated gold ring highlight (`0 0 0 2px rgba(184, 151, 88, 0.3)`).

### Service Mega-Menu & Content Cards
- **Mega-Menu Grid:** Multi-column layout divided by fine vertical rules (`1px solid rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`). Category headings feature small line-drawn icons and uppercase golden overlines.
- **Service & Mentorship Cards:** Elevated cards bordered with 1px hairpins, featuring an EB Garamond title, bulleted milestone checkmarks, and institutional status chips (e.g., "ABNT 2024 Em Conformidade", "Sigilo Garantido").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Style:** Square (checkbox) or circular (radio) controls with 1px midnight borders, filling with burnished bronze (`#B89758`) and white checks upon selection.
